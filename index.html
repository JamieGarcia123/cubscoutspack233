BSA SCOUT TROOP 233
Leon Springs, Texas

Adventure. Leadership. Service. Life Skills.

Since 1966, Troop 233 has helped Scouts in Northwest San Antonio grow through unforgettable outdoor adventures, hands-on learning, community service, and leadership opportunities. We believe the best way to prepare young people for life is by giving them the confidence to lead, explore, and serve other.

Meeting Time

Our combined (boy and girl) troop meets every Monday - 7:00 p.m. to 8:30 p.m (No regular Troop meetings the Monday after Campouts, Spring Break, Summer Break, or Christmas Break)
