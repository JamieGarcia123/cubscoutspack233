Welcome to Scouting BSA.

We are Troop 233 – award-winning High Adventure Troops located in Northwest San Antonio, Leon Springs area since 1966. We have found that high adventure and variety of activities helps with scouts and adults to maintain long lasting interest in scouting.

Meeting Time

Our combined (boy and girl) troop meets every Monday - 7:00 p.m. to 8:30 p.m (No regular Troop meetings the Monday after Campouts, Spring Break, Summer Break, or Christmas Break)
